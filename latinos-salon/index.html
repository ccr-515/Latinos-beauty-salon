About Latino’s Beauty Salon

Latino’s Beauty Salon is a family-owned salon and barbershop that has been part of the 6th Avenue corridor in Des Moines for over 20 years. We’re a bilingual team (English & Spanish) and we specialize in haircuts for men, women, and kids, plus color, highlights, fades, brow shaping, and facial waxing.

We’re proud to serve the neighborhood with professional work at fair prices and a welcoming, relaxed atmosphere. Whether you’re coming in for a quick fade, a full color change, or getting the kids ready for school photos, we treat every client like family.
